Freight Tracking & Logistics

Find answers to common questions regarding real-time tracking, delivery status updates, and route management.

Our real-time tracking utilizes advanced GPS and cellular network triangulation, updated every 30 seconds. In urban areas, accuracy is typically within 10 meters. In remote logistics corridors, intermittent connectivity may cause brief delays, but the system will automatically sync once a connection is re-established.
Route changes can be initiated by the Logistics Controller via the central dashboard. Once approved, the new route is instantly pushed to the driver's mobile terminal. The system automatically recalculates estimated arrival times and updates all stakeholders. Note that significant deviations may incur additional fuel surcharges as per the contract terms.

Status badges provide quick visual indicators of your freight's current state:

  • PENDING - Awaiting dispatch confirmation.
  • IN TRANSIT - Vehicle is actively moving towards the destination.
  • DELAYED - Temporary hold due to traffic, weather, or border checks.
  • DELIVERED - Unloading complete and signed off.

If tracking is lost for more than 45 minutes, our system automatically flags the shipment as 'Signal Lost'. First, check if the vehicle is traversing a known dead zone (e.g., specific dense forest regions or deep valleys). If the loss persists, contact Customer Support immediately using the 'Urgent Inquiry' button below.
